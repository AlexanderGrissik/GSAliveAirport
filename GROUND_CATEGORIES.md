# Ground SimObject Categories

The generated SimObject catalog contains the following useful ground-service categories. FSDT contributes thousands of company and airport livery variants, so the entries below are grouped into model families.

## 1. Workers and people

- Marshallers: `FSDT_Marshaller_01` through `FSDT_Marshaller_06`
- Wing walkers: `FSDT_Wingwalker_Male_*` and `FSDT_Wingwalker_Female_*`
- Baggage workers: `FSDT_Baggage_Loader_Man_*`
- Catering workers: `FSDT_catering_man_*`
- Stair operators and vehicle drivers
- Loadmasters and attendants
- Cleaning crews: `FSDT_cleaning_crew_*`

## 2. Baggage carts and dollies

- `ASO_Baggage_Cart01`
- `ASO_Baggage_Cart02`
- PMDG baggage-cart sets

## 3. Baggage tractors and trucks

- `ASO_BaggageTruck01`
- PMDG baggage tractors

## 4. Baggage belt loaders

- `Aso_Baggage_Loader_01`
- FSDT baggage-loader families, including versions with workers

## 5. Cargo equipment

- FSDT cargo loaders
- Wide/main-deck cargo loaders
- Cargo-configured baggage loaders and tugs
- Loadmaster workers

## 6. Passenger stairs and ramps

- `ASO_Boarding_Stairs`
- Red and yellow Asobo variants
- Several FSDT staircase types
- Aviramp models
- Stair operators and drivers

## 7. Catering trucks

- `ASO_Catering_Truck_01`
- `FSDT_Catering_01`
- `FSDT_Catering_EU`
- Many regional and company livery variants

## 8. Ground power units

- `Car Ground Power Unit`
- `FSDT_GPU_Hobart_4400`
- `FSDT_GPU_TLD_406`
- PMDG GPU models

## 9. Pushback tractors and apron tugs

- `ASO_Pushback_Blue`
- `ASO_Pushback_White`
- `ASO_Tug01_White`
- `ASO_Tug02_White`
- Multiple FSDT pushback and tug sizes
- Some FSDT models have separate driver objects

## 10. Passenger buses and shuttles

- `ASO_Shuttle_01`
- `Bus Apron 02`
- `FSDT_PassengerShuttle`
- FSDT Cobus and Neoplan buses

## 11. Fuel vehicles

- FSDT fuel-hydrant dispensers
- Fuel trucks and regional livery variants

## 12. Potable-water vehicles

- `FSDT_Water_Truck`
- `FSDT_Water_Truck_Man`
- `FSDT_Water_Truck_Woman`
- Microsoft water trucks

## 13. Lavatory vehicles

- `FSDT_Lavatory_Truck`
- Microsoft lavatory trucks and vans
- PMDG lavatory truck

## 14. Deicing vehicles

- `FSDT_DeIce_Safeaero_220`
- Microsoft large and small deicing trucks

## 15. Follow-me and airport-operations vehicles

- `FSDT_FollowMe_class_B`
- Many airport-specific variants

## 16. Utility and maintenance vehicles

- `ASO_CarUtility01`
- `ASO_TruckUtility01`
- PMDG maintenance van
- Regional utility trucks

## 17. Safety and apron equipment

- Passenger barriers
- Various GSX positioning and pointer objects

## 18. Fire and rescue

- `ASO_Firetruck02`
- FSDT Panther fire trucks
- Microsoft airport fire trucks

## Jetways

Jetway-related entries were returned, but they are primarily FSDT GSX pointer/helper objects rather than useful standalone jetways. Jetways are excluded from the planned service design.
